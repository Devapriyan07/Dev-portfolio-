# Assets Folder

Place your files here:

## Required files:

1. `photo.jpg` — Your professional photo
   - The hero portrait will display this image
   - Recommended: high-quality portrait, ideally 600x800px or similar
   - A placeholder is shown if the file is missing

2. `resume.pdf` — Your Resume PDF
   - The "VIEW RESUME" button at the bottom of the Contact section links to this file
   - Place your actual resume PDF here with exactly the filename: resume.pdf

## After adding your files:
- Refresh index.html in your browser — the photo and resume button will work automatically.
